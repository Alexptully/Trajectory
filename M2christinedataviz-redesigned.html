The data demonstrates a clear positive correlation between SAT and PSAT means. Schools that scored well in the PSAT in Grade 11 would often score just as well in the SAT.

Figure 1.3
The most notable aspect of the comparison between the school year tested and the ERW mean was during the 2019 to 2020 year, where the mean dropped significantly. Given the global circumstances at the time, it reflects that students graduating that year weren’t given as many resources and opportunities that would’ve otherwise improved their chance of success. Additionally, the chart follows a general pattern where the score rises for a few years before dipping and then rising again, showing that students are continually improving.
