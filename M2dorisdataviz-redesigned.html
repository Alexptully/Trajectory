Figure 1.1
Seven of the ten schools gained more in math. Math programs are a useful starting point for your research.

Tolland has the largest math increase in this group: 86 points, compared with 59 in reading and writing.

Capital Preparatory Harbor shows a different pattern: reading and writing increased 91 points, compared with 43 in math. Researching its literacy instruction could be especially useful. Farmington's gains are nearly balanced: 70 points in math and 68 in reading and writing.

Figure 1.2
Tolland had almost the same gain in both classes: +72 and +73 points. That makes it interesting for researching practices that may support repeated improvement.

Trumbull and Joel Barlow repeated their gains exactly: Trumbull gained 67 points in each class, and Joel Barlow gained 55.

Farmington had the largest increase between classes: its PSAT-to-SAT gain rose from +80 to +114 points. This school can be researched further to see if anything changed in instruction or support.

The chart includes multiple examples of different schools with classes graduating the same year and their results differ.
